The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on #4471 writes the day first. Dates come out with day and month swapped. This is the fix already queued.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants on booking-v3 type month first, as told, and the form reads it day first. Same symptom, different bug: it needs the hint fixed, not the parser.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, not by a swap (“Tuesday 12 May… Monday 11 May”, “only June”, “Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”). The desk already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). It points at a time zone conversion.

How the reports were placed

By tenant, channel and form, with causeOf in causes.ts, the same as the #4471 table, so the counts are the desk’s. A report the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) stays counted where it is filed and is footnoted as shift. Two things were not used:

  • Subject lines. Every subject on a hint or shift report also appears on parser reports, so decision 0001, which merges on subject alone, put all three problems in one ticket.
  • Intake tags. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser. The tag disagrees with the tenant’s cause on dozens of reports.

Lessons

  • A count is only as good as the merge. Read a sample of bodies before you merge, not after.
  • Same symptom is not same cause. Merge on cause; subject is a hint, not a key.
